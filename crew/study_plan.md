## Day 1 – Get the Big Picture  

| Time | Activity | What to Do |
|------|----------|------------|
| 9 am – 9:30 am | **Set a goal** | Write down one clear goal for the next three days (e.g., “understand the main ideas of the topic”). |
| 9:30 am – 10:30 am | **Read an overview** | Skim a textbook chapter, a reliable website, or a video that gives a broad introduction. Highlight any words you don’t know. |
| 10:30 am – 10:45 am | **Break** | Stretch, drink water. |
| 10:45 am – 12:00 pm | **Make a mind‑map** | Put the main idea in the centre and draw branches for the big sub‑topics you just saw. |
| 12:00 pm – 1 pm | **Lunch break** | Relax! |
| 1 pm – 2 pm | **Glossary building** | Look up the unknown words from the overview and write simple definitions. |
| 2 pm – 3 pm | **Quick recap** | Explain the main idea and the sub‑topics out loud, as if you’re teaching a friend. |
| 3 pm – 3:15 pm | **Break** | Walk around. |
| 3:15 pm – 4 pm | **Set up study tools** | Create a folder (digital or paper) for notes, flashcards, and practice questions. |

---

## Day 2 – Dive Deeper  

| Time | Activity | What to Do |
|------|----------|------------|
| 9 am – 9:15 am | **Review Day 1** | Look at your mind‑map and glossary. |
| 9:15 am – 10:30 am | **Focused reading** | Choose one sub‑topic from the map. Read it more carefully, taking short notes (one sentence per idea). |
| 10:30 am – 10:45 am | **Break** | Light snack. |
| 10:45 am – 12 pm | **Create flashcards** | On one side write a term or question; on the other side write the simple answer. Make at least 5–7 cards. |
| 12 pm – 1 pm | **Lunch** | |
| 1 pm – 2 pm | **Practice with flashcards** | Test yourself, saying the answer out loud. Shuffle the cards after each round. |
| 2 pm – 3 pm | **Apply the idea** | Write a short paragraph (3‑4 sentences) that uses the new information in a real‑life example. |
| 3 pm – 3:15 pm | **Break** | |
| 3:15 pm – 4 pm | **Summarise** | Add a new branch to your mind‑map with the details you just learned. |

---

## Day 3 – Review & Test Yourself  

| Time | Activity | What to Do |
|------|----------|------------|
| 9 am – 9:30 am | **Quick mind‑map scan** | Look at the whole map and say each branch out loud. |
| 9:30 am – 10:30 am | **Flashcard review** | Go through all cards twice, focusing on any you got wrong. |
| 10:30 am – 10:45 am | **Break** | |
| 10:45 am – 12 pm | **Take the quiz** | Answer the five questions below (no notes allowed). |
| 12 pm – 1 pm | **Lunch** | |
| 1 pm – 2 pm | **Check answers** | Compare your answers with the key and note any gaps. |
| 2 pm – 3 pm | **Fix the gaps** | Re‑read the sections you missed and add a new flashcard for each. |
| 3 pm – 3:15 pm | **Break** | |
| 3:15 pm – 4 pm | **Final reflection** | Write 3 sentences about what you learned and how you will keep using it. |

## Quick 5‑Question Quiz  

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | What is the purpose of a mind‑map in the first day of study? | To visualise the main idea and its big sub‑topics, helping you see how everything connects. |
| 2 | When you create flashcards, what should be on each side? | One side has a term or question; the other side has a short, simple answer. |
| 3 | After reading a sub‑topic in depth, what short writing task helps you apply the knowledge? | Write a brief paragraph (3‑4 sentences) that uses the new information in a real‑life example. |
| 4 | During the final review, what two activities are most important for solidifying memory? | Re‑going through flashcards and taking a short, timed quiz without notes. |
| 5 | How many times should you shuffle your flashcards during a study session? | At least after each round of testing, to keep the order unpredictable. |

*Use the quiz to see how well you’ve remembered the key study habits. Good luck!*
